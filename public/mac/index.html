Built for people who get email as files: claims, legal and IT teams.

Space bar previews

Select a .msg in Finder and press Space. Inline images, tables and headers render, with the inspector one click away.

An inspector that doesn't guess

See the route a message recorded, its sender authentication results, attachments and links. It reports what the file says; it never issues a verdict.

Whole mailboxes

Drop in an .mbox, an Apple Mail mailbox, a folder of messages or a Google Takeout export. Search across all of it, with previews on every row.

Offline by design

Sandboxed, with parsing in its own isolated process. Mail never leaves the Mac, and there's no telemetry. Remote images never load.

Questions

Does Mailviewer upload my mail?

No. Mailviewer for Mac makes no network connections at all: messages are read in a separate sandboxed process, nothing is uploaded, and it never checks for updates. Your IT team updates it with the tools it already uses.

How is the license delivered?

One key covers your organization: the key names your organization and, for Team and Business, how many Macs the plan covers. Push it to every Mac in one configuration profile, or paste it once into Settings. Replacing, loaning or re-imaging a Mac needs nothing from you or from us. Keys are checked offline.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The free web app at mailviewer.app opens the same formats in your browser, and nothing leaves your computer there either.
